## Anatomy

<DropdownMenuDemo />

In a table this is the kebab in an `ActionCell`, which is where most of them live.
The menu handles roving focus and type-ahead, so arrow keys and typing the first
letter both work without anything extra.

## Use something else when

- **There are two actions and room for both.** Show them. A menu that hides two
  buttons costs a click and reveals nothing.
- **It is a value being chosen, not an action performed.** `Select` for a short
  list, `Combobox` when it needs searching. A menu does something; a select sets
  something.
- **It is a single destructive action.** A `destructive` button with a
  confirmation, so the consequence is visible before the click rather than after
  it.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Put destructive items last, under a `DropdownMenuSeparator`.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Mix a delete in among ordinary actions, where a mis-click lands on it.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Label items with the verb: "Assign wallets", "Suspend".
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Label them with the object alone. "Wallets" does not say what happens.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="side" type="'top' | 'right' | 'bottom' | 'left'" default="'bottom'">
    Preferred side. Flips when there is no room.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="align" type="'start' | 'center' | 'end'" default="'center'">
    Alignment along that side. `end` for a kebab at the right edge of a row.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="DropdownMenuItem disabled" type="boolean" default="false">
    Dims and skips it in keyboard navigation.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
