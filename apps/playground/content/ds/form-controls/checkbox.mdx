## States

<CheckboxStatesDemo />

Checkbox and `Switch` share their focus, disabled and checked tokens, so the two
read as siblings. What differs is meaning, not appearance.

## Use something else when

- **The setting applies immediately.** `Switch`. A switch turns something on and
  it stays that way; a checkbox records an answer that gets submitted with a form.
- **There is one choice from several.** Radio buttons, or `Select` when the list
  is long enough to need a menu.
- **The reader is filtering a list.** `FilterChip` through `SearchBar`, not a
  column of checkboxes.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do example={<span className="flex items-center gap-2.5"><Checkbox id="cb-a" /><Label htmlFor="cb-a">I confirm the beneficiary details are correct</Label></span>}>
      Use a checkbox for consent, acknowledgement, or an answer that travels with
      a form.
    </Do>
    <Dont example={<span className="flex items-center gap-2.5"><Checkbox id="cb-b" defaultChecked /><Label htmlFor="cb-b">Dark mode</Label></span>}>
      Use a checkbox for a setting that takes effect the moment it changes. That
      is a `Switch`.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do example={<span className="flex items-center gap-2.5"><Checkbox id="cb-c" /><Label htmlFor="cb-c">Send a copy to the operator</Label></span>}>
      Write the label as what checking it does.
    </Do>
    <Dont example={<span className="flex items-center gap-2.5"><Checkbox id="cb-d" /><Label htmlFor="cb-d">Do not skip notification</Label></span>}>
      Write a label that is true when unchecked. A negated checkbox takes two
      readings to understand.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="checked" type="boolean">
    Controlled state. Pair with `onCheckedChange`.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="onCheckedChange" type="(checked: boolean) => void">
    Fires on change.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="defaultChecked" type="boolean" default="false">
    Uncontrolled starting state.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="disabled" type="boolean" default="false">
    Removes pointer events and drops opacity. A bound `Label` dims with it.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="aria-invalid" type="boolean">
    Draws the error border, for a required consent that was not given.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
