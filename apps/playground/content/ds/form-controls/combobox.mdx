## Anatomy

<ComboboxDemo />

The chips composition above is the multi-select form: `ComboboxChips` holds the
chosen values and the input, and doubles as the anchor the list hangs off.

`ComboboxContent` takes an **`anchor` ref**, and it is required rather than
optional. Combobox is built on Base UI while the rest of the overlay components
are Radix, so it does not inherit the anchoring the others get for free. A
combobox rendered without `anchor` opens in the wrong place, and nothing warns
you.

## Use something else when

- **The list is short and fixed.** `Select`. A search field over five options is
  furniture.
- **The options are actions.** `DropdownMenu`.
- **The reader is narrowing a table.** `SearchBar` with `filterCategories`, which
  renders the filter builder and keeps the query in the URL.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Pass an `anchor` ref to `ComboboxContent`, taken from `ComboboxChips` or the
      trigger.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Omit it and assume the list will find its own position. This is the one
      component in the DS that cannot.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Change the placeholder once something is chosen: "Select roles" becomes
      "Add role".
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Keep the empty-state prompt after the field has values in it.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="multiple" type="boolean" default="false">
    Turns `value` into an array and enables the chips composition.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="value / onValueChange" type="string | string[] / (value) => void">
    Controlled pair. The array form pairs with `multiple`.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="ComboboxContent anchor" type="RefObject<HTMLElement>">
    What the list positions against. Required.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="ComboboxItem value" type="string">
    What gets stored. The children are what the reader sees.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
