## Anatomy

The same three parts as `Input`: a bound `Label`, the field, and a line of help or
error text.

<Anatomy>
  <Part name="Label">
    Bound with `htmlFor`.
  </Part>
  <Part name="Textarea">
    The field. Grows with its content via `field-sizing-content`, from a
    four-line minimum.
  </Part>
  <Part name="Help text" optional>
    One line under the field. Becomes the error message when invalid.
  </Part>
</Anatomy>

## States

<TextareaStatesDemo />

The field grows as it fills rather than scrolling inside a fixed box, so the
reader can see everything they have written. It does not shrink below its
four-line minimum.

## Use something else when

- **The value fits on one line.** `Input`. A textarea for a name promises more
  room than the value needs.
- **The value comes from a known set.** `Select` or `Combobox`.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do example={<div className="w-full max-w-56 space-y-1.5"><Label htmlFor="ta-a">Reason</Label><Textarea id="ta-a" placeholder="Explain what the operator should fix." /></div>}>
      Use a textarea where the answer is a sentence someone writes.
    </Do>
    <Dont example={<div className="w-full max-w-56 space-y-1.5"><Label htmlFor="ta-b">Bank code</Label><Textarea id="ta-b" placeholder="007" /></div>}>
      Use a textarea for a short fixed value. The size of the box is a promise
      about the length of the answer.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do example={<p className="text-caption text-[var(--color-text-muted)]">Shown to the participant who submitted the request.</p>}>
      Say who will read it, when the text is going somewhere.
    </Do>
    <Dont example={<p className="text-caption text-[var(--color-text-muted)]">Enter a reason</p>}>
      Restate the label as help text.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

`Textarea` takes every attribute of a native `textarea`.

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="aria-invalid" type="boolean">
    Draws the error border and ring. Pair it with a message in the help line.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="disabled" type="boolean" default="false">
    Removes pointer events and drops opacity.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="rows" type="number">
    Sets the starting height. The field still grows past it.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
