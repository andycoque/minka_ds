## Anatomy

<SectionIntroDemo />

Replaces a section's content the first time someone arrives: a lightbulb, a welcome
heading, the section's summary, three cards, and one call to action.

The cards should explain the **mental model**, not tour the tabs. Tabs are discoverable on
their own; the model is not. Each card pairs a mock of what the reader is about to see with
one concept and its consequence.

## Variants

None. What varies is the three cards.

## Element variations

Card mocks should be built from the **real components** the reader will meet, not shapes
that resemble them. The purpose of a card is recognition when the section fades in behind
it, and an approximation teaches a layout that does not exist.

A mock may animate when the idea is a change rather than a state: a balance that rises and
falls needs motion, a table does not. Prefer one animated card in a set — three competing
loops fight for the attention the copy needs.

## Motion

Entry staggers title, then bulb and summary together, then each card, then the button, on
`transition-delay` rather than chained timers so a slow render cannot desync it.

On continue, three separated beats:

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0 → 900ms | The bulb travels to the guide launcher's corner and settles |
| 900ms | The launcher's chrome appears around it, as a square, with a dot texture spreading behind |
| 1250ms | Its icon and label follow, and the square widens to fit them |
| 1950ms | The section is handed back and fades in |

That last gap is the point. Handing back as the label appears drops the content on top of
a gesture still in progress and cuts the arrival off mid-sentence.

The bulb is measured, not assumed: it queries
`[data-slot="page-help-launcher"]` for its target, because the launcher is fixed to the
viewport with responsive insets. With no launcher on the route, the flight is skipped
rather than animating to nowhere.

## Use something else when

- **The section reads as what it is.** A list of users is a list of users. A blocking
  takeover before every table is a toll, not an onboarding.
- **Only one concept needs explaining.** A guide topic, or a `HelpExpander`.

Reserve this for sections a first-time reader would **misread**, not merely find
unfamiliar: money appearing from nowhere, a credential that is not a person, two different
things both called keys.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Gate it per section, and let a preview query parameter force it open so it can be
      reviewed without clearing storage.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Write the dismissal flag in preview mode. It then shows once and cannot be seen
      again without devtools.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Let the arrival finish before handing the section back, and fade the content in.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Swap the takeover for the content directly. With no entry transition it appears at
      full opacity instantly, which reads as a jump however well the flight is timed.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="title" type="string">
    The section name as the nav calls it. Prefixed with a dimmed "Welcome to".
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="summary" type="string">
    Reuses the guide's summary, so the two say the same thing. Split at the first colon.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="cards" type="SectionIntroCard[]">
    Three reads best; more and the row stops scanning.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="onLauncherStage" type="(stage) => void">
    Drives the guide launcher's staged reveal. A callback rather than a context read, so
    the component does not need to know how an app wires its shell.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="onDone" type="() => void">
    Called once the arrival has settled.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
