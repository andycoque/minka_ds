## Anatomy

The default trigger is a circular question mark beside a heading, which is where all
six studio usages put it.

## Variants

Two modes, and the choice is about what is around the trigger.

`popover` floats the card beside the trigger and is portaled, so it works anywhere,
including inside a dialog or a table cell. Use it unless you have a reason not to.

`inset` is the exception, and a narrow one: see below.

`inset` expands the card inside the nearest positioned container and frosts what is
behind it.

In practice it means **one place: the left panel of a dialog or a wizard.** All six uses
in the product are there, sitting below the panel's preview or illustration and filling
the space underneath it. Four are in dialogs, two in wizards.

That is not a coincidence about where it happens to be used. `inset` expands into the
nearest positioned container, so it needs one with room to grow into, and a side panel is
the only surface that reliably has both: it is tall, it is not where the reader is
typing, and the space under a preview card is otherwise dead. Elsewhere the card either
has nowhere to expand or covers the fields being filled in.

All six pass `anchor="bottom-right"`, which is already the default and can be omitted.

## Use something else when

- **It is one line.** `Tooltip`. Opening a card to read six words is a poor trade.
- **The reader needs it to finish the task.** Put it on the page. Anything behind a
  trigger is optional by construction.
- **It is a whole section that collapses.** `ExpandablePanel`.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Explain a concept the reader might not share: what quorum means, why a
      period has a limit.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Restate the label. If the field name needs explaining, fix the field name.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Use `inset` in the left panel of a dialog or wizard, under the preview. That is
      what it was built for and where all six uses live.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Use `inset` anywhere else. Outside a panel there is no positioned container with
      room, so the card either cannot expand or covers what the reader is using.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="title" type="string">
    The card heading.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="children" type="ReactNode">
    The explanation.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="mode" type="'popover' | 'inset'" default="'popover'">
    See [Variants](#variants).
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="anchor" type="'bottom-right' | 'bottom-left' | 'top-right' | 'top-left'" default="'bottom-right'">
    Which corner of the panel an `inset` card grows out of. Every studio use takes the
    default.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="trigger" type="{ icon?: ReactNode; label?: string }">
    Overrides the default question mark. A `label` makes it a text trigger.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
