## Anatomy

<PageHelpDemo />

A launcher pinned to the bottom-right of the content area, and a panel that grows out of
it. The panel carries a title, a summary, and two collapsible sections: **Concepts** (the
vocabulary the page assumes) and **What you can do** (the actions available here).

Labelled **Guide** with a lightbulb, not "Help" with a question mark. A `?` in a corner is
the universal support affordance, and this panel reaches no one: it explains the page the
reader is already on. Promising support and delivering a glossary is worse than not
offering it.

## Variants

There are none. The panel's shape is fixed; what varies is the content passed to it.

Two properties change what appears:

- `docHref` adds a link out to full documentation, pinned below the scroll region.
- `highlight` marks the launcher with a dot until it has been opened once. A marker rather
  than a timed tooltip, because the reader may look away at the moment a tooltip would
  appear.

## Element variations

A topic can carry two optional extras, and both are earned rather than default.

`icon` — only when the topic already **has** an established glyph elsewhere in the
product, so the reader meets the same mark here and on the control they are about to use.
That is recognition. A glyph invented for a concept ("what is quorum?") is decoration, and
it competes with the serif title for the same job.

`action` — a way to go and do the thing just explained. Rendered at the end of the
expanded body, never on the collapsed row: on the row it makes the panel a second launcher
competing with the page's own toolbar, but after the explanation it reads as "now that you
know what this is, here is the door".

## Motion

The panel scales from `0.95` and rises `8px` out of the launcher's corner over 200ms, and
the launcher is replaced by it rather than sitting alongside, so the button reads as
becoming the panel.

Topics reveal with a `grid-template-rows` collapse from `0fr` to `1fr` rather than a
conditional render. A mounted element has no start state to transition from, so a
conditional render can only pop.

## Use something else when

- **It explains one control.** `HelpExpander`, beside the control. This panel is for
  orientation before the reader has understood the page at all.
- **The reader needs it to finish the task.** Put it on the page. Anything behind a
  launcher is optional by construction.
- **There is nothing to define.** A section whose content is self-evident should render no
  launcher. An absent button beats one that opens to say "this is the overview".

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Title topics with words the reader has seen on screen: "Why does a movement need
      approval?"
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Ask "What is quorum?" — a title naming the term it defines is useless to anyone who
      does not already know it, so they never open it.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Keep the content registry as data, resolving actions by key at render time.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Put handlers in the registry. An unwired key should render no button, which is what
      lets coverage grow one section at a time.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="title" type="string">
    The panel heading. "About &lt;thing the reader sees&gt;", matching the nav.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="summary" type="ReactNode">
    One or two sentences on what the page is for.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="concepts" type="HelpTopic[]">
    Vocabulary the page assumes the reader already has.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="actions" type="HelpTopic[]">
    What the reader can do here.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="highlight" type="boolean" default="false">
    Marks the launcher until the panel has been opened once.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="onOpen" type="() => void">
    Fired on first open, so a caller can clear `highlight`.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="launcherReveal" type="'hidden' | 'chrome' | 'full'" default="'full'">
    Staged reveal, for when a `SectionIntro` animates into this position.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="docHref" type="string">
    Optional link to full documentation.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
