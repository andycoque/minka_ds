The collapsed row has to be enough. If a reader must open the panel to know whether they
want it open, the summary has failed — that is what `subtitle` and `meta` are for.

## Use something else when

- **The content is short enough to just show.** Show it. A panel that everyone opens is
  a click charged for nothing.
- **It is a section of a detail page.** [Section card](/docs/ds/surfaces/cards). Sections
  are not hidden by default.
- **It is one of several exclusive views.** [Tabs](/docs/ds/navigation/tabs).

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Say what is inside: "4 fields", "3 events".
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Label it "Details" with nothing else. The reader has to open it to find out whether
      they needed to.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="title" type="ReactNode">
    Required. Readable while collapsed.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="subtitle" type="ReactNode">
    The summary that saves opening it.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="meta" type="ReactNode">
    Right-aligned: a count, a timestamp.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="open" type="boolean">
    Controlled. Omit for uncontrolled.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
