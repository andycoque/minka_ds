## Anatomy

<FlowDiagramDemo />

Two nodes, an amount, and a direction. It answers the question a top-up or withdrawal
dialog exists to answer: what moves, from where to where, and what are the balances
afterwards.

**Vertical and two-ended, on purpose.** For a horizontal route with several hops and a
notion of where it stopped, that is
[Process diagram](/docs/ds/diagrams/process-diagram) — a sibling, not a variant of this.
This one has no per-node state because a balance movement either happens or does not;
"where did it stop" is not a question a top-up asks.

## Element variations

**The nodes do not swap when the direction changes.** `top` stays on top. Only the arrow
and its sheen reverse. That is deliberate: a diagram whose boxes move when you toggle a
radio makes the reader re-read it every time.

**`amount: 0` renders the idle state**, not a zero. Before anything is entered, the
diagram shows the shape of the movement without pretending a figure exists.

**`after` on each node** is the projected balance. Passing `null` leaves it off, for a
flow where the outcome is not yet known.

**`accent`** tints the arrow with a brand pair. Semantic only where the movement itself
carries meaning, as on issue and destroy — a top-up between two participants is not a
different colour from a withdrawal.

## Use something else when

- **There is one object, not a movement.** [Diagram node](/docs/ds/diagrams/diagram-node)
  or an object card.
- **The path has more than two ends, or can stop partway.**
  [Process diagram](/docs/ds/diagrams/process-diagram).
- **It is a sequence of events over time.** [Timeline](/docs/ds/diagrams/timeline).

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Show `after` balances once the amount is known, so the consequence is visible
      before the reader commits.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Show only the current balances. The reader then does the arithmetic that the
      diagram exists to do.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="top / bottom" type="FlowNode">
    Required. `{ name, current, after }`, plus an optional subtitle and icon.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="amount" type="number">
    Required. Zero renders the idle state.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="direction" type="'up' | 'down'">
    Required. Which way the arrow travels. Node positions do not change.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="currency" type="string">
    Unit shown beside the amount.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="format" type="(n: number) => string">
    Custom formatter. Defaults to `$` plus en-US grouping.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="footer" type="ReactNode">
    Content below the diagram, e.g. a reference pill.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
