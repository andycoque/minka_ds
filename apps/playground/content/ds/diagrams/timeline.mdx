## Anatomy

<TimelineDemo />

Events down a rail, grouped into sections. Each section is a **phase** of the record's
life with a milestone naming it, so the reader can follow the shape of what happened
without reading every row.

Used by the transaction lifecycle, where the question is usually "how far did this get,
and where did it stop".

## Element variations

**`items`** are the events: a label, a status, an optional actor in `meta` and a
timestamp. An item with `fields` can expand to show the underlying proof, so the detail
is available without being in the way.

**`milestone`** names the phase. `active: false` dims it, for a phase not yet reached.
`position` decides which end of the items it sits at — every section in the product
passes `top`, so the phase name leads the events it covers.

**`annotationBefore`** puts an alert inside the rail, before a section's items. For
something that explains the section rather than being an event in it.

**`truncateAtFailure`** stops rendering after the first failure. The steps that never ran
are not shown as pending, because they are not pending — they are moot, and showing them
greyed implies they are still coming.

## Use something else when

- **The events have no phases.** A plain list. The rail and milestones cost vertical
  space that only pays off when there is a structure to show.
- **It is a form with steps.** `Wizard`, or `Stepper` for a progress indicator.
- **It is two balances and a movement.** [Flow diagram](/docs/ds/diagrams/flow-diagram).

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Put the actor in `meta` and the time in `timestamp`, so both columns stay
      scannable down the rail.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Write them into the label. "Ledger created the transaction at 14:00" cannot be
      scanned against the row above it.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Set `truncateAtFailure` on a record that stopped.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Render the remaining steps as pending. They will never run, and pending says they
      might.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="sections" type="TimelineSection[]">
    Required. Each takes `items`, a `milestone` and a `position`.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="truncateAtFailure" type="boolean" default="false">
    Stop after the first failed item and its section.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
