## Use something else when

- **The reader has to answer before continuing.** `Dialog`. A sheet leaves the
  page usable behind it, which is the opposite of a decision point.
- **The detail deserves a URL.** A detail page. Anything worth linking to or
  reloading should not live in a panel that closes.
- **It is a short form on one record.** `Dialog`, which centres the attention it
  is asking for.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Use a sheet for detail the reader compares against the list behind it.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Use one for a confirmation. Nothing about a sheet says the page is blocked,
      because it is not.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Keep `side="right"`, so every sheet in the product arrives from the same
      edge.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Vary the side per screen. The direction becomes noise rather than meaning.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
