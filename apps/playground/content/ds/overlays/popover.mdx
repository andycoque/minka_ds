## Use something else when

- **It is a list of actions.** `DropdownMenu`, which handles roving focus and
  keyboard selection.
- **It is one line explaining a control.** `Tooltip`.
- **It needs a decision before the reader continues.** `Dialog`. A popover
  dismisses when you click away, so it cannot hold anything that must be
  answered.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Keep it to one job: a short form, a filter, a piece of detail.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Put a multi-step flow in a popover. There is no room and nothing anchors
      the reader if it closes.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Anchor it to the control it belongs to, so the connection is obvious.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Open it far from its trigger. A floating surface with no visible source
      reads as an error.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="side" type="'top' | 'right' | 'bottom' | 'left'" default="'bottom'">
    Preferred side. Flips when there is no room.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="align" type="'start' | 'center' | 'end'" default="'center'">
    Alignment along that side.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="open / onOpenChange" type="boolean / (open: boolean) => void">
    Controlled pair. Omit both to let the trigger manage it.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
