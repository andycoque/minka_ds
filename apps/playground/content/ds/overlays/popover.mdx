## Motion

<AnchoredLoop />

The trigger stays put and the panel moves against it, because that relationship *is* the
animation. The panel slides 8px **from the trigger**, which is what makes it read as
attached rather than as something that happened to appear nearby.

The loop walks all four sides. Note the slide always comes from the trigger's edge, and
that it follows the **resolved** side rather than the requested one — a popover that
flipped near a screen edge still animates from where it actually ended up.

Nothing sets a duration: these are `tw-animate-css` utilities, and 150ms `ease` is that
library's default rather than a decision of ours.

Tooltip, DropdownMenu and Select pass the same four classes, so the family reads as one
thing:

```
animate-in fade-in-0 zoom-in-95 slide-in-from-{side}-2
```

## Use something else when

- **It is a list of actions.** `DropdownMenu`, which handles roving focus and
  keyboard selection.
- **It is one line explaining a control.** `Tooltip`.
- **It needs a decision before the reader continues.** `Dialog`. A popover
  dismisses when you click away, so it cannot hold anything that must be
  answered.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Keep it to one job: a short form, a filter, a piece of detail.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Put a multi-step flow in a popover. There is no room and nothing anchors
      the reader if it closes.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Anchor it to the control it belongs to, so the connection is obvious.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Open it far from its trigger. A floating surface with no visible source
      reads as an error.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="side" type="'top' | 'right' | 'bottom' | 'left'" default="'bottom'">
    Preferred side. Flips when there is no room.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="align" type="'start' | 'center' | 'end'" default="'center'">
    Alignment along that side.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="open / onOpenChange" type="boolean / (open: boolean) => void">
    Controlled pair. Omit both to let the trigger manage it.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
