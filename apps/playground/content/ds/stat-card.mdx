## Variants

`type` is a discriminated union, so the props change with it rather than every
card carrying every field.

| Type | For | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `count` | A number of things | `number \| null` |
| `amount` | Money | `string \| number \| null`, plus `unit` |
| `status` | A state, not a figure | `status` string plus `color` |

`count` is the default and needs no `type`.

A `null` value renders a dash, not a zero. The distinction matters on a ledger:
zero transactions is a fact, and not knowing yet is not.

## Use something else when

- **The figure needs a trend line.** A chart. A stat card carries a single
  `percent` against the previous period and nothing more.
- **It is a state rather than a summary.** `type="status"` covers the card case;
  in a table row or a card summary it is `StatusCell`.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Give the card an `onClick` when the figure corresponds to a filter, so the
      reader can act on what they just read.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Make a card clickable when nothing happens. It becomes a button that lies.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
  <Rule>
    <Do>
      Pass `unit` for money, so the currency sits beside the figure.
    </Do>
    <Dont>
      Bake the currency into the value string. It then scales with the number and
      competes with it.
    </Dont>
  </Rule>
</DoDont>

## Props

<PropsTable>
  <Prop name="type" type="'count' | 'amount' | 'status'" default="'count'">
    Picks the shape of the rest of the props.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="label" type="string">
    What the figure is. Required on all three types.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="value" type="number | string | null">
    `count` and `amount`. `null` renders a dash.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="unit" type="string">
    `amount` only. The currency code.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="percent" type="number">
    Change against the previous period. Signed.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="status / color" type="string / 'success' | 'error' | 'muted'">
    `status` only.
  </Prop>
  <Prop name="onClick" type="() => void">
    Makes the card a button.
  </Prop>
</PropsTable>
